An offline, ad-free reader for Android built in Flutter. Supports PDFs, EPUBs,
Kindle formats, comic archives, and office documents with a dedicated reflow
engine that renders reflowed pages in clean typography and warm reading themes.
Includes reading insights, highlight cards, bookmarks, a sleep timer, and
text-to-speech without any WebView. Operates fully on device with no INTERNET
permission, reading files through the Android Storage Access Framework.
