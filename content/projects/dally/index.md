A minimal, 100% offline games collection application published on the Google Play Store. Built with Flutter, Dart, and Riverpod, Dally features classic games such as Chess, Sudoku, 2048, Solitaire, Ludo, Snakes & Ladders, and Minesweeper. It requires zero network permissions, stores all stats and progress locally on the device, and collects no telemetry or analytics.
