A calm RSS reader for Android built to be a finite briefing: it reads the feeds you
choose in the order they were published, groups them into your own categories, and
ends in "You're caught up". Includes a one-item-per-screen Linger mode, an in-app
reader with on-device article extraction, feed discovery from a pasted site address,
OPML import/export, and a stats screen that is a mirror, not a scoreboard. Local-first
with no account, server or analytics.
